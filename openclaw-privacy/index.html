Privacy Policy - OpenClaw Personal Assistant

This is a private personal-use application operated by Catalin Carstea. It accesses Google Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, and Drive only to perform tasks requested by the account owner, including reading and organizing email, managing calendar information, resolving contacts, and retrieving files.

OAuth credentials and retrieved information are stored locally on the owner's computer. Data is not sold, advertised against, or shared with third parties except as required to provide the requested Google services. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.
