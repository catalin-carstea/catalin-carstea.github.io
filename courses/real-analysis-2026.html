Course description

Measure theory and Lebesgue integration, from Euclidean spaces to abstract measure spaces. Emphasis on proofs, examples, and applications.

Prerequisites. Introductory analysis or advanced calculus; Riemann integration, basic metric spaces, and proof writing.
